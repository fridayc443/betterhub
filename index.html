About BetterHub

BetterHub is a Nigerian digital services marketplace. We work with several suppliers behind the scenes, so you deal with one account, one wallet and one support team.

We set our own prices, keep every transaction on record, and refund orders that fail. When a supplier is slow or unavailable, we reconcile the order before refunding, so you are not charged twice or left without a result.

FAQs

What can I use virtual numbers for?

Receiving SMS verification codes for your own accounts. Do not use them for fraud, impersonation or anything that breaks a platform’s rules.

How do I pay?

Fund your wallet in Naira, then every order draws from that balance. Funding goes through Paystack, and your balance updates as soon as the payment is confirmed.

What if my order fails?

If the order definitively fails, the amount returns to your wallet and you get a notification. If the supplier is just slow, we check the order status first before refunding.

Can I get a refund after I receive my code?

No. Once a code is delivered the service has been used. Cancelled orders with no code are refunded.

Is my wallet balance safe?

Only our servers can change balances, and every change is written to a permanent record you can view in your transaction history.

How do I contact support?

Message us on WhatsApp from the Contact page.
